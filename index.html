How net is worked out: gross USD − receiving fee % − fixed fee per payment, then × the mid-market rate, then − PayPal's conversion spread. The spread is the all-in gap between mid-market and what lands in your bank; PayPal quotes "4% above base rate" but its base rate already sits below mid-market, so real-world spreads run higher. Calibrating from a real transfer and a real payment is the reliable way to set both numbers; recheck every few months. Settings and data are saved in this browser.
